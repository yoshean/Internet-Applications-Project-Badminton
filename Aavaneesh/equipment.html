Badminton Equipments

These are the badminton equipments for you to bring:
1)You need the badminton racket
2)You need a shuttlecock
3)bring a non marking shoes
4)Bring a water or a sports drink

Why each equipment is so important to badminton:
1)badminton racket-Rackets vary in weight, balance point, and shaft flexibility. A head-heavy racket provides more power for smashes, while a head-light racket offers faster reaction times for defensive play. Choosing the right racket prevents wrist fatigue and matches your personal playing style.
2)shuttlecock-The projectile used in the game, available in feathered or synthetic variants.
3)Non marking shoes-these shoes has a grip and movement but also court protection and safety for yourself.
4a)Water-its the best for training and matches
4b)Sports drink- can provide a quick enegy drink carbohydrates
Important Note:Both water and sports drink are allowed,make sure dont leave in the badminton court.
