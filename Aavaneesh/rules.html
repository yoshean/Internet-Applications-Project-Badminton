Rules and Regulations for Badminton

What are The rules and Regulations?


a)Scoring and Matches:
i)The first side to win two games wins the match
ii)in every single game, a point is scored when the shuttlecock reached the other player's side
iii)In every game the player should lead two points(21-19) but if the players are draw like 29-29 then 30th points wins
iv)in each game,when the player lead 11 points then it will lead a 2 minute break

b)Serving rules:
i)The serve should be cross the court in the oppenets's service box.
ii)The shuttlecock must struck the server's waist from the below
iii)the server and the receiver ca be remain in contact using both feet during the service
iv)the player should serve in the right side if the score is even (0,2,4) but the player score is odd(1,3,5) then the player should serve in the left side
